## 1. Executive Summary & Confirmatory Verdict

This report presents the empirical execution of the three alternative evaluation designs formally pre-declared in `docs/V22_EVAL_PROTOCOL.md` and frozen under tag `modeling-spec-frozen`. 

### Core Research Question
Does monetary policy tightening by the world's leading central banks transfer predictably and stably into international equity index declines?

### Empirical Verdict: NOT SUPPORTED
Across all three pre-declared designs—regime-stratified forward chaining, leave-one-tightening-episode-out (LOTEO), and the restated v1 blind test—the empirical data **fail to reject the null hypothesis of zero tightening transfer** after robust wild cluster bootstrap inference and multiplicity adjustments. Out-of-sample predictability is consistently non-positive ($R^2_{\text{OOS}} \le 0$), directional prediction accuracy is no better than random guessing (39%–50%), and point estimates are highly sensitive to single extreme events.

## 2. Design 1: Regime-Stratified Forward Chaining

Models are estimated with and without the single most influential event (`cbr_2014-12-16`, 650 bp policy rate hike, +499 bp yield proxy surprise, Tier 2).

All calculations are generated by `scripts/run_v22_declared_designs.py` and deposited in `outputs/modeling/v22_design1_forward_chaining.csv`.

### 2.3 What Forward Chaining Can and Cannot Say
- **What It Can Say**: As training samples accumulate across 2011–2023, the estimated sensitivity of equity returns to monetary policy surprises in clean Tier 1 intraday shocks remains consistently close to zero ($\hat{\beta} \approx +0.0027$ to $+0.0036$). In no forward-chaining fold is the coefficient negative or statistically significant ($p_{\text{wild}} \in [0.166, 0.534]$). Furthermore, out-of-sample directional prediction accuracy is 39.3% in 2018, 47.2% in 2019, and 50.5% in 2023—demonstrating zero out-of-sample predictive power.
- **What It Cannot Say**: It cannot rule out very small effects ($< 83$ bp for Tier 1, $< 37$ bp for un-leveraged Pooled), as statistical power in expanding slices is constrained by the small number of rate hikes in the early years.
- **Dimension Classification**: **NOT SUPPORTED** (Minimum Detectable Effects range from 11.8 bp to 175.1 bp).

## 3. Design 2: Leave-One-Tightening-Episode-Out (LOTEO)

### 3.1 Protocol and Pre-Declared Decision Rule
Monetary tightening is highly clustered in macroeconomic episodes. Standard temporal splitting risks interpolating within an active tightening cycle. LOTEO holds out entire tightening episodes:
- **Episode 1 ($E_1$, 2011)**: ECB Trichet hikes (April and July 2011).
- **Episode 2 ($E_2$, 2015–2018)**: Federal Reserve post-ZIRP tightening cycle (9 rate hikes).
- **Episode 3 ($E_3$, 2022–2023)**: Synchronized global inflation response (Fed, ECB, BoE, BoC, RBA rapid hikes).

**Pre-Registered Decision Criterion**:  
> *"A negative equity transmission response ($\beta < 0$) must persist out-of-episode across at least 2 of the 3 episodes to be considered structurally stable."*

All calculations are generated from `scripts/run_v22_declared_designs.py` (`outputs/modeling/v22_design2_loteo.csv`).

### 3.3 What LOTEO Can and Cannot Say
- **What It Can Say**: The pre-registered decision criterion **FAILS**. A negative equity transmission response does not persist across episodes:
  1. In $E_1$ (2011), the equity reaction was positive ($\hat{\beta}_{\text{holdout}} = +0.0090$).
  2. In $E_2$ (2015–2018), while $\hat{\beta}_{\text{holdout}} = -0.0064$, the training model fitted without $E_2$ predicts out-of-episode returns with negative $R^2$ ($R^2_{\text{OOS}} = -0.0398$) and sub-50% directional accuracy (49.0%).
  3. In $E_3$ (2022–2023), $\hat{\beta}_{\text{holdout}} = -0.0010$ ($p_{\text{wild}} = 0.5986$), $R^2_{\text{OOS}} = -0.1326$, and directional accuracy is 46.8%.
- **What It Cannot Say**: It cannot claim that monetary policy never affects equity markets; it establishes that a simple linear transmission model estimated on historical monetary policy surprises cannot reliably forecast cross-episode tightening reactions.
- **Dimension Classification**: **NOT SUPPORTED / INCONCLUSIVE** (Structural stability: Not Supported; Statistical power constrained: MDE 17.8 bp to 109.9 bp).

## 4. Design 3: Formal Restatement of v1 Blind Test Results

### 4.1 Specification Difference & Hierarchy

$$\text{clr}_W = \alpha + \beta \cdot \text{shock\_std} + \gamma \cdot \text{overlap\_Wd} + \epsilon$$
The historical v1 blind test execution (`src/modeling/run_v1_blind_test.py`) inadvertently omitted `overlap_Wd`, executing a bivariate specification ($\text{clr}_W \sim \text{shock\_std}$).

Neither the confirmatory primary specification nor the labelled deviation meets the pre-registered success criteria: no horizon exhibits a statistically significant negative coefficient after Holm family-wise multiplicity adjustment across the five horizons ($p_{\text{Holm}} = 1.0000$ for all horizons).

- **Influence Sensitivity**: `cbr_2014-12-16` is a Tier 2 event (Bank of Russia); the v1 blind test evaluated strictly Tier 1 high-frequency announcements for the Federal Reserve, ECB, and Bank of Japan, meaning `cbr_2014-12-16` is completely absent from the v1 sample by construction.
- **Dimension Classification**: **NOT SUPPORTED (Confirmatory Empirical Null)**.
